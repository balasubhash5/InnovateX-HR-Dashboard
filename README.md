# InnovateX-HR-Dashboard
Developed an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard using Power BI to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, salary trends, department performance, and employee satisfaction indicators. Used KPIs, charts, and tables to identify workforce patterns, compare salaries, and support data-driven HR decisions.
# 👥 InnovateX HR Analytics Dashboard | Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview
The InnovateX HR Analytics Dashboard is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze workforce data, employee attrition, salary patterns, and departmental performance. It provides HR insights through KPIs, charts, tables, and interactive filters to support workforce planning and data-driven decision-making.

## 🎯 Project Objectives
- Analyze total employees and employee attrition.
- Examine average salary, hourly rate, and salary hike percentage.
- Explore workforce demographics across different age groups.
- Compare attrition across departments.
- Analyze employee performance ratings by job role.
- Compare average monthly income by job role and gender.
- Identify the top 10 highest-paid employees.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies
- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and reporting
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – KPI calculations and measures
- **Data Visualization** – KPI cards, donut charts, bar charts, scatter plots, and tables

## 📊 Dashboard Features

### 1. HR Key Performance Indicators
Displays total attrition, total employees, average hourly rate, average salary hike, and average salary.

### 2. Workforce Demographics
Analyzes employee distribution across age groups, including under 25, 25–34, 35–45, and over 45, with gender-based comparisons.

### 3. Department-Wise Attrition
Visualizes employee attrition across departments to identify areas that may require further investigation.

### 4. Attrition by Performance Rating
Compares employee counts across job roles and performance ratings.

### 5. Salary Analysis by Job Role and Gender
Compares average monthly income across job roles and genders.

### 6. Salary Hike and Attrition Analysis
Explores the relationship between employee attrition status and salary hike percentages.

### 7. Top 10 Highest-Paid Employees
Displays the highest-paid employees based on monthly income.

### 8. Interactive Filtering
Includes a marital status slicer to explore employee data across selected groups.

## 💡 Business Benefits
- Helps HR teams monitor workforce metrics.
- Supports employee attrition analysis.
- Enables salary and compensation comparisons.
- Provides insights into workforce demographics.
- Helps identify departments for further HR investigation.
- Supports workforce planning and informed decision-making.
